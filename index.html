ComfyUI node pack · by Promptus

Clone or design a voice on your own PC

ComfyUI-ZONOS3 brings Zyphra's free, open ZONOS3-Mini voice model into ComfyUI. Clone a voice from a short clip, shape a brand new one with sliders, or pick one of 27 built-in voices, in 43 languages.

Get the node pack Run it in Promptus

Five nodes

ZONOS3 Server

Connects to Zyphra's local server, and starts it for you when you give it the server's folder.

ZONOS3 Voice

One of 27 built-in voices, or a clone from any audio clip. Up to 60 seconds is used.

ZONOS3 Voice Blend

Morph smoothly between two built-in voices.

ZONOS3 Voice Design

25 sliders for who, texture, room, delivery and mood, plus 15 accents and 8 presets.

ZONOS3 Text to Speech

Text in, standard ComfyUI audio out, with a quality guard that retakes skipped words.

Two ready-made templates

Clone Your Voice

Load 10–30 seconds of your own voice, type a script, press Run.

Design a Voice

Start from a built-in voice or a preset and move the sliders.

Why a server?

ZONOS3-Mini runs in Zyphra's own local server rather than inside ComfyUI. It pins its own CUDA kernels and audio libraries, and loading those into ComfyUI would break other node packs. Zyphra's servers for Windows, Linux and Mac all speak the same API, so one node pack works everywhere.

Get started

  1. Clone this repo into ComfyUI/custom_nodes and restart ComfyUI.
  2. Build Zyphra's ggml server once and download the model (about 1.2 GB). The README has the exact commands.
  3. On ZONOS3 Server, set zonos3_folder to the ZONOS3-Mini folder. Open a template and press Run.

Good to know

Best clips

10–30 seconds of one person speaking clearly, no music.

Go easy on stacking

Liveliness, Happy and Chatty together raise the pitch a lot. A little goes a long way.

Consent

Clone your own voice, or voices you have written permission to use.
